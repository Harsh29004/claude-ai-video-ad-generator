# SnackSmart: brief (user-provided)
SnackSmart is a US grocery-list app that scans your pantry with the phone camera and suggests healthy snacks you can make from what you already have. Free, iOS and Android.
Top angle: "Busy parents throw away food they forgot they bought, then order takeout anyway."
Campaign name: "Use What You've Got". CTA: "Download SnackSmart free".
